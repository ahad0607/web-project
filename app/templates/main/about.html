About LifeBridge

LifeBridge: Blood Donation & Thalassemia Care Management System is an integrated healthcare platform designed to solve two deeply interlinked crises: acute emergency blood shortages and chronic thalassemia transfusion management.

Understanding Thalassemia in Pakistan

Pakistan carries one of the highest burdens of Beta Thalassemia Major worldwide, with an estimated 5,000–9,000 children born with the disorder each year. Due to severe genetic defects in hemoglobin synthesis, these children suffer from profound life-threatening anemia and require:

  • Regular Blood Transfusions: Typically every 2 to 4 weeks throughout life to sustain minimum hemoglobin levels (>9.5 g/dL).
  • Iron Overload & Chelation Monitoring: Each transfusion deposits iron that the human body cannot excrete naturally. Continuous serum ferritin tracking and daily iron chelation therapies (such as Deferasirox) are essential to prevent fatal cardiac and endocrine failure.
  • Emergency Donor Shortages: Unplanned blood shortages often delay scheduled transfusions, causing irreversible pediatric organ damage.

Core Pillars of LifeBridge

Automated Emergency SOS

Algorithmic matching of compatible, eligible blood donors within locality and instant notification dispatch.

Thalassemia Transfusion Cycles

Automated 2-4 week recurring schedule calculator, linked blood requisitioning, and pre/post transfusion lab tracking.

Cold-Chain Blood Bank Inventory

Unit-by-unit bag tracking (PRBC, Platelets, FFP), expiration warnings, and stock transfer ledgers.

Gamified Donor Retention

Dynamic milestone badges, 90-day cooldown enforcement, and university donation drives.
